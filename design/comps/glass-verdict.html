1 — Full glassmorphism

Glass everywhere, with a saturated aurora field behind it so the blur has actual material to refract. This is the only condition where full glass earns its cost — without that color field, glass over black is just grey at 5% opacity.

2 — Selective glass (recommended)

Cards are near-opaque ink panels with a 1px gradient edge — crisp, fast, numbers always legible. Glass is reserved for the few surfaces that genuinely float over content: nav pill, sheets, dialogs, sticky headers. There it blurs real scrolling content, which is what glass is for.

3 — No glass at all

Pure solid panels, flat borders, no ambient glow, no blur anywhere. Maximum performance and contrast — but the UI loses its atmosphere; nothing feels layered or lit. This is "clean" in the forgettable sense.
