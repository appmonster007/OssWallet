A / B / H — Light & Dark

Each direction must hold its personality in both modes. Light modes are not inverted darks — they're re-lit: A-dark becomes charcoal paper with sage accent; B-light becomes a laboratory panel with volt darkened to olive (chartreuse is illegible on white); H-light becomes ivory with the champagne deepened to bronze. The floating element above the nav in each frame is your collapsed filter pill (summoned by tapping the card's period readout; expands into the full filter sheet) — it follows the selective-glass rule: it floats over content, so it's a glass surface.

Each frame: hero · cash-flow card (period-only readout) · collapsed filter pill.
