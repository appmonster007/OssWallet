OssWallet is a privacy-first, encrypted, and highly customizable personal finance manager.

All data is stored locally in a SQLCipher-encrypted (256-bit AES) database — nothing leaves your device except the network requests listed below.

Features:

* End-to-end local encryption of all financial data
* Multi-profile support to isolate separate sets of accounts and transactions
* Planned payments with automated recurrence scheduling
* Full-text search across transactions
* Customizable dashboard with a card-based layout
* Open in Web: an embedded local server to manage your data from a desktop browser on the same network
* Budgets, loans, and account structures for cash, bank, and credit accounts
* Currency conversion using live exchange rates (requires internet access; optional)

Network use (the only times the app goes online):

* Exchange rates: only when you tap refresh in Settings > Currency, or if you turn on "Update Rates Automatically" (off by default). Rates come from the provider you picked (open.er-api.com or api.frankfurter.dev); only the currency code is sent. Nothing is contacted during setup.
* Location picker: when you open the map or search for a place on a transaction, map tiles load from tile.openstreetmap.org and addresses are looked up through nominatim.openstreetmap.org.

AI assistant access (Android 16+) is off by default. If you turn it on in Settings > Security & Privacy, system assistants can read and add transactions, and the assistant may process that data off your device.

OssWallet does not require a Google account, does not use any proprietary Google Play Services APIs in this build, and does not include ads or trackers.
