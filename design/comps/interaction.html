Card interaction model — capabilities without chrome

Every card keeps its two capabilities — filter logic and configuration — but the buttons are gone. The card's own anatomy becomes the interface: the period readout is the door to filtering, and the card's back side is the door to configuration. Resting state shows the time period only; the filter set stays out of sight.

1Resting state

Clean card: title, time period readout (always visible, gold), data. No buttons, no filter summary. The dotted underline on the readout is the only hint that it's interactive.

2Tap the period readout

A single tap on the readout unfolds the query editor inline — period, tags, categories as removable pills, "+ Filter" to add logic. Tap the readout again (or scroll away) and it folds back up.

3Press & hold the card

A long-press (~450ms) anywhere on the card body scales it down slightly, then flips it over in 3D — like turning a physical card to read its back. Haptic tick at the moment of commit.

4The configuration back

The card's reverse side is the config surface — same footprint, gold-tinted frame. Toggles for data options, style picker, remove-card. "Done" (or back gesture) flips it home.
